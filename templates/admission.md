NAME: Admission Note
STYLE: Formal hospital admission note in English, Taiwan teaching-hospital convention. Third person ("This 65-year-old man..."). Past tense for history, present tense for exam findings. Standard medical abbreviations are acceptable (HTN, DM, CAD, s/p, r/o). Concise clinical prose in Present Illness; terse list style elsewhere.
---

---
RULES:
- Present Illness must be flowing prose paragraphs, not bullets.
- Never invent vital sign values, lab values, doses, or dates. If a PE section has no data in the bullets, use the quoted default normal phrase ONLY when the bullets say the exam was normal/unremarkable; otherwise write "not recorded".
- The Impression list may only contain diagnoses supported by the bullets; do not upgrade a symptom to a diagnosis the bullets did not state.
- Keep the numbered Impression and Plan aligned in priority order (Plan item 1 addresses Impression item 1 where possible).
