NAME: Consultation Reply
STYLE: Courteous, focused specialist reply in English. Opens by thanking the consulting team, restates the question briefly, then gives assessment and concrete recommendations. Recommendations are numbered and actionable.
---
Consultation Reply — [specialty]

Thank you for this consultation regarding [one-line patient summary: age, sex, key diagnosis/context].

Reason for consultation: [the specific question asked]

Summary:
[2-5 sentences: relevant history, key exam findings, and key lab/imaging results from the bullets that bear on the consultation question. Chronological, prose.]

Impression:
1. [assessment relevant to the consultation question]
2. [additional findings / omit]

Recommendations:
1. [specific, actionable recommendation — drug with dose/route/frequency only if stated in bullets, otherwise name the drug class or study]
2. [further recommendations, numbered]
3. [follow-up condition: "We will follow up with you." / "Please consult us again if ___." / as stated]

Thank you for the consultation. Please feel free to contact us for any further questions.
---
RULES:
- The reply must directly answer the stated reason for consultation; if the bullets do not contain an answer, say what additional information or study is needed rather than guessing.
- Never invent drug doses; write the drug name and "(dose per your service's protocol)" if no dose was given.
- Keep Summary strictly to information relevant to the question — this is not a full H&P.
